Creating & Managing Fundraising Campaigns

Comprehensive guide to setting up and managing fundraising campaigns.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

The Campaigns module is where you create, view, and manage our fundraising initiatives.

What Is a Campaign?

A campaign determines where a donation will be allocated within your project. Examples of campaigns include Zakat al-Mal, Zakat al-Fitr, Masjid Renovations, Sadaqah, and other fundraising initiatives.

Once a campaign has been created and enabled, it becomes available across MAC's donation platforms, allowing donors to contribute to that specific cause through multiple channels, including:

  • The Donation Portal (donations.macnet.ca)
  • MAC Donation Kiosks
  • MAC POS (S700)

Using campaigns ensures that donations are accurately categorized, reported, and directed to the intended fundraising initiative.

Creating a New Campaign

To create a new donation campaign, navigate to Administration â†’ Campaigns.

On this page, you'll see a list of all campaigns that have already been created for your centre. To create a new campaign, click the + (Add) icon in the top-right corner.

Figure: The Add Campaign form with all required fields for creating a new fundraising campaign.

Complete the following fields:

  • Campaign Type –Select Donation.
  • Campaign Name –Enter the name of your campaign (e.g., Zakat al-Mal, Masjid Renovations, Sadaqah, etc.).
  • Description –Provide a brief description of the campaign. This description may be displayed to donors on supported donation platforms.
  • Campaign Identifier –This field links your campaign to the appropriate accounting category, ensuring donations are recorded correctly for financial reporting. If you cannot find the appropriate campaign identifier, check the "I'm unable to find my campaign" box. This will notify the Accounting team, who will create and assign the correct identifier.
  • Suggested Amounts –Enter preset donation amounts that will be displayed to donors, making it easier for them to choose a contribution amount.
  • Recurrence Frequency –Select which recurring donation options you would like to offer: One-Time, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
  • Visible On –Choose where the campaign should appear: Donation Portal, Donation Kiosks, or MAC POS (S700).
  • Status –Set the campaign to Active to make it available to donors or Inactive to hide it without deleting it.
  • Campaign Icon –Upload an icon or image to visually represent the campaign where supported.

Once all required fields have been completed, click Save to create the campaign. If the campaign is marked as Active and enabled for one or more platforms, it will automatically become available on the selected donation channels.

Editing Campaigns

  1. Select the campaign from your dashboard.
  2. Click the blue Edit button to open the settings.
  3. Update your information and click Save to publish your changes.
