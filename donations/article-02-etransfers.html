E-Transfers

Learn how to manage and verify direct bank transfer donations.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

Automated Workflow

When a donor sends an Interac e-Transfer to a MAC centre, the system automatically sends an email inviting them to create a donor profile in MACSIS.

Once the donor creates their profile, their donation history will be linked to their account, allowing them to track their contributions and become eligible to receive tax receipts for verified donations. Any future e-Transfer donations made using the same email address will also be automatically associated with their donor profile, providing a seamless and convenient donation experience. If a donor uses a different email, they must link their email to their account.

How Do I View My e-Transfers?

To view all e-Transfer donations, navigate to Finance then Donations, and finally e-Transfers.

The e-Transfers page displays all Interac e-Transfer donations received through MACSIS beginning in 2026, when the feature was introduced. From this page, you can review and manage each donation before it is verified.

For each e-Transfer, the following information is displayed:

  • Donor Name
  • Email Address
  • Donation Amount
  • Transaction Date
  • Reference Number
  • Campaign Message (if the donor included one with their e-Transfer)

This page provides a centralized view of all incoming e-Transfer donations, making it easy to track, verify, and manage donations across your MAC centre.

How Do I Add a Campaign to an e-Transfer Donation?

It is the responsibility of the centre manager or administrator to assign a campaign to each e-Transfer donation. If a donor did not specify a campaign in their e-Transfer message, you should assign the appropriate campaign before verifying the donation.

To assign a campaign:

  1. Navigate to Finance then Donations and finally e-Transfers.
  2. Select the checkbox next to the donation you wish to update. You may select multiple donations to perform a bulk update.
  3. From the top toolbar, click the Select Campaign dropdown.
  4. Choose the appropriate campaign from the list of available campaigns for your centre.
  5. Click Apply to assign the selected campaign to the chosen donation(s).

The selected campaign will be applied to all checked donations, making it easy to update multiple e-Transfer donations at once.

Note

Please ask donors to include a message with their e-Transfer indicating the campaign they wish to support (e.g., Zakat al-Mal, Zakat al-Fitr, Masjid Renovations, etc.).

If no campaign is specified in the e-Transfer message, the donation will automatically be processed as a General Donation.

Important:

Donors will not receive a tax receipt from their e-transfers if they do not complete their donor profile.

When an e-transfer is processed, the banking system only provides the donor's name, email address, and the donation amount. It does not transmit the donor's mailing address.

Because a complete residential address is required to issue an official charitable tax receipt, donors must click the link in their automated confirmation email to create their profile and provide their full address. Without this step, no tax receipt can be generated.
