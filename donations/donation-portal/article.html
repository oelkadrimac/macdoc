Donation Portal

Online donation portal for donors to make contributions.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

The Donation Portal serves as the primary external web platform where donors can choose their nearest MAC location, select specific campaigns to support, and securely submit their contributions.

Location, Campaign Selection & Contributions

The portal offers a streamlined browsing and checkout experience for community members:

  • MAC Location Selection: Donors begin by choosing their nearest MAC centre or chapter location to ensure funds are properly allocated.
  • Campaign Selection: After selecting a location, donors choose specific campaigns (such as Zakat al-Mal, Masjid Renovations, or General funds) to support.
  • Flexible Giving Options: Donors can choose to make one-time gifts or set up automated recurring subscriptions on a monthly or yearly basis.
  • Secure Checkout: Donors enter their payment details directly within the portal, ensuring all transaction data is processed securely.

Confirmation & Profile Setup Workflow

Once a contribution is successfully submitted, the system triggers a streamlined onboarding and profile management sequence:

  • Confirmation Email: The donor immediately receives a confirmation email acknowledging their gift.
  • Profile Creation Link: Included within the email is a unique link prompting the donor to set up or access their portal profile where they can manage their payments.

Automatic Synchronization with MACSIS

All financial contributions made through the portal sync automatically with the MACSIS backend:

  • CC Donations Dashboard: All portal donations are routed and recorded directly under the CC Donations dashboard.
  • Administrative Tagging: Every entry is automatically tagged with structured remarks (e.g., [Donation portal]-[Donation]-[General]-[MAC Toronto]) to ensure clean tracking and auditing across different MAC centres.
  • CT Number Generation: Administrators can review portal donations in the backend dashboard and click the Get CT Number button to generate and assign official tracking numbers.
