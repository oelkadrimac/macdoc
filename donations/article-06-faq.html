FAQ

Frequently asked questions about donations and campaigns in MACSIS.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

1. Dashboard Navigation & Channel Sources

Where do our donations come from across MAC?

MACSIS centralizes contributions from the Donation Portal (donations.macnet.ca), Donation Kiosks at participating centres, and in-person MAC POS (S700) terminals into a single management hub.

How do I access CC Donations and e-Transfers?

Sign in at sis3admin.macnet.ca, expand the Finance tab (marked with the `$` icon) in the left sidebar, and click Donations to access CC Donations, e-Transfers, and related modules.

2. Managing e-Transfers and Campaigns

How do I assign a campaign to an e-Transfer donation?

Navigate to Finance > Donations > e-Transfers, select the target donation(s) via checkboxes, choose the campaign from the top toolbar's Select Campaign dropdown, and click Apply.

How do I create a new fundraising campaign?

Go to Administration > Campaigns, click the `+` (Add) icon in the top-right corner, fill out the required campaign details (such as name, type, identifier, and recurrence), and click Save.

3. Refunds, Subscriptions, and Tax Receipts

What is the refund process for credit card donations?

For transactions within the 30-day window, navigate to Finance > Donations > CC Donations, click the transaction ID link, open the Payments tab, click the blue Refund button, complete the details, and click Apply.

How do I identify recurring subscriptions?

Recurring plans can be monitored under Finance > Subscriptions. They feature a distinct Wi-Fi icon next to their ID and a numerical multiplier indicating billing frequency.

How do donors become eligible to receive tax receipts?

Donors become eligible to receive tax receipts for verified donations once they create a profile and link their donation history to their account.

4. General Troubleshooting & FAQs

What happens if a donor does not specify a campaign in their e-Transfer message?

The donation will automatically be processed as a General Donation. Administrators should review the e-Transfer entry and manually assign the appropriate campaign before verifying the deposit.

What should I do if I cannot find the appropriate Campaign Identifier?

Check the box labeled "I'm unable to find my campaign" during campaign creation. This notifies the Accounting team, who will create and assign the correct identifier centrally.

How are refund requests handled after the 30-day window?

Once 30 days have passed, transactions can no longer be refunded directly through the dashboard. The donor must contact the MAC team directly for assistance.
