Refunds

Learn how to process and manage donation refunds in MACSIS.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

The refunds workflow allows administrators to process valid returns for credit card donations directly through the MACSIS backend when a donor requests a refund due to accidental duplicate charges, incorrect campaign allocations, or other valid circumstances.

Refund Eligibility & Window

Donation refunds are subject to strict operational timeframes and rules:

  • 30-Day Limit: Refunds are only eligible within 30 days of the original transaction date.
  • Expired Windows: Once 30 days have passed, transactions can no longer be refunded directly through the dashboard. Donors requesting refunds after this period must contact the MAC team directly for assistance.

How Do I Process a Refund?

To issue a refund for an eligible transaction, follow these steps:

  1. Navigate to Finance → Donations → CC Donations in the sidebar menu.
  2. Locate the target transaction within the table and click on the transaction ID link to open the comprehensive Donation Detail view.
  3. Select the Payments tab within the detail page to view payment entries.
  4. Click the blue Refund button located in the Actions column.
  5. In the Refund pop-up window, complete the required fields:
    • Refund Amount: Verify or adjust the numerical amount to be refunded.
    • Refunded By: Enter the name of the administrator processing the request.
    • Remarks: Enter any necessary notes, test details, or reasons for the return.
  6. Click Apply to finalize the transaction refund.

Important

Refund processing times depend on the original payment gateway provider. Credit card processors typically take 5 to 10 business days for the funds to reflect back onto the donor's card.
