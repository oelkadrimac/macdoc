01 Managing Credit Card (CC) Donations

Donations Module –“ CC Donations

The Donations module, located under the Finance tab, serves as the central hub for viewing, filtering, and tracking all financial contributions. The CC Donations section specifically displays all online credit card transactions processed through the donation platform.

Accessing the Donations Module

  1. Sign in to the MACSIS Admin Panel using your Microsoft MAC email account: https://sis3admin.macnet.ca/home
  2. Once you are signed in, locate the Finance tab in the left-hand navigation menu (identified by the $ icon) and expand it.
  3. Click Donations to view the available options:
    • CC Donations
    • E-Transfers

CC Donations

The CC Donations section displays all credit card donations received through the following sources:

  • Donation Kiosks (if your centre has kiosks installed)
  • Online Donation Portal (donations.macnet.ca)
  • MAC POS Reader (S700) (if your centre uses an S700 payment terminal)

All credit card donations from these sources are consolidated into the CC Donations section, making it easy to search, filter, and track donation activity in one place.

Features & Navigation

  • Total Donations Badge: A green summary badge showing the total value of credit card donations based on your current filters.
  • Filter Subscription Type: Sort by monthly or one-time gifts to identify recurring donations.
  • Filter Campaign Type: View funds attributed to a specific initiative or the general pool.
  • Search Bar: Quickly find specific donations by ID, CT Number, name, email, or phone number.
  • Sorting: Sort the data table by any column (such as date or amount) by hovering over the header and clicking the sorting arrow.

Data Table Attributes

IDThe unique identification number for the transaction.
Campaign NameThe specific fund or initiative the donor supported.
AmountThe total dollar value of the processed transaction.
Donor InfoContact details provided by the donor at checkout.
DateThe exact date and time the transaction occurred.
CT NumberThe internal audit or campaign tracking number.

Viewing Donation Details

Clicking on the Donation ID of any row will redirect you to the Donation Detail page. This page contains two key tabs:

  • Payments Tab: Offers more details on the payment and transaction, including a button to refund that donation. For subscription-type donations, this tab displays all payments relevant to the subscription. One-time donations only display one row.
  • Refunds Tab: Provides detailed information on each refund applied to the donations shown in the Payments tab, allowing you to track all refund activity for a specific donation.

Figure: The Donation Detail page displays donor information at the top, with the Payments and Refunds tabs below for transaction management.

02 Reconciling & Verifying e-Transfers

The e-Transfer tab helps you manage direct bank transfers. Because these are initiated through banking portals rather than an automated gateway, this section allows you to manually verify deposits against our bank records.

Automated Workflow

  1. When a donor sends an e-Transfer, they are emailed an invitation to create a donor profile.
  2. You will simultaneously receive a notification email that a new e-Transfer is pending.
  3. The transaction appears in the SIS backend, ready for you to review and verify.

Available Features

  • Filter & Add Campaign: Use this to filter by initiative or manually assign a campaign to a transfer if the donor didn't select one.
  • Search: Locate deposits using the bank–™s Reference Number, donor name, or email.
  • Bulk Actions: Use checkboxes to select multiple transactions and apply campaign updates at once.

Key Banking Data

Reference NumberThe unique confirmation code from the bank –” essential for verifying successful deposits.
MessageNotes left by the donor, often including their email or intended campaign.
Account NameThe legal name on the bank account, critical for reconciling business or spousal transfers.
03 Creating & Managing Fundraising Campaigns

The Campaigns module is where you create, view, and manage our fundraising initiatives.

Dashboard Tips

  • Search: Find campaigns by name or project.
  • Filter Status: Toggle between active and inactive campaigns.
  • Carry-Forward: Easily migrate legacy campaigns from previous years to the current fiscal year.

Creating a New Campaign

Note: If you see an alert that "No campaigns found in Business Central," please contact the accounting team to set up the core records before proceeding.

Figure: The Add Campaign form with all required fields for creating a new fundraising campaign.

Click the blue '+' button to open the form. Key fields include:

  • Type: Choose Donation or Sale.
  • Name/Description: Provide a clear, public-facing title and summary.
  • Identifier: Select the correct internal audit code.
  • Icon: A graphic is mandatory; missing this will cause a submission error.
  • Status: Check 'Active' to publish; leave unchecked to save as a draft.

Editing Campaigns

  1. Select the campaign from your dashboard.
  2. Click the blue Edit button to open the settings.
  3. Update your information and click Save to publish your changes.
