MAC POS

Point-of-sale system for in-person donations and transactions.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

Point of Sale (POS) machines are used at MAC centers to quickly collect in-person credit and debit card donations. Understanding how these machines work helps ensure that physical donations are properly recorded and donors get their tax receipts.

POS Hardware Supported

MAC uses handheld card readers to process these in-person transactions:

  • Clover Machines: Older physical payment terminals used across various centers.
  • Stripe S-700 Terminals: Newer card readers built by Stripe that run a custom MAC donation app (called Cloak).

Why POS Donations Are Anonymous

When someone donates using a POS machine, they just tap their card and get a printed paper receipt. The machine does not ask for their personal information or mailing address. Because of this, POS donations are completely anonymous, and the system does not automatically know who made them. To issue a tax receipt, we need to connect the donor's full name, email, and address to that specific payment.

How to Process POS Donations for Tax Receipts

To make sure donors get their tax receipts in February, center staff must use physical pledge forms:

  • Step 1 - Fill Out the Form: Ask the donor to completely fill out a center pledge form with their name, email, phone number, and address.
  • Step 2 - Attach the Receipt: Print the paper receipt from the Clover or Stripe machine and staple it to the donor's completed pledge form.
  • Step 3 - Send to Accounting: Give all completed pledge forms with the stapled receipts to the MAC accounting team.
  • Step 4 - Manual Entry: Accounting will manually enter this information into the system so the donation is linked to the donor's profile, allowing them to get their tax receipt.
