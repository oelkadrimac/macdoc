MAC POS

Point-of-sale system for in-person donations and transactions.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

MAC Point of Sale (POS) machines are used at MAC centers to quickly collect in-person credit and debit card donations.

Hardware Supported

For MAC POS, we utilize Stripe S-700 Terminals for in-person transactions. These S-700 machines are directly connected to MACSIS, meaning donations processed through them will appear in the system.

Processing Donations & Tax Receipts

Unlike older physical systems, the S-700 terminals handle tax receipt information digitally:

  • No Paper Receipts: The Stripe S-700 machines do not print physical paper receipts.
  • Email Prompt: When processing a donation, the terminal has a section that asks the donor to enter their email address.
  • Profile Creation: If the donor provides their email address, they will receive an email containing a link to create a donor profile.
  • Tax Receipts: In order to receive an official tax receipt, the donor must follow that link and complete their profile setup so their information is properly tracked in MACSIS.

Clover Machines

If your center is using older Clover machines, please refer to the Clover POS Documentation. Clover machines use paper receipts and physical information forms because they are not connected to MACSIS.
