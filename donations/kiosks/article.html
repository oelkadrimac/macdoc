Kiosks

Manage self-service donation kiosks and track kiosk transactions.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

MAC Donation Kiosks are large, standalone donation machines found in the head office and MAC mosques. They let people easily make self-service donations by tapping their cards. It is important to understand how they work so you can help donors fix missing tax receipts.

How Donating at a Kiosk Works

When someone uses a kiosk, the process looks like this:

  • Step 1 - Choose a Campaign: The donor picks what they want to support on the screen.
  • Step 2 - Tap to Pay: The donor taps their credit or debit card on the machine to donate.
  • Step 3 - Enter Email: After the payment goes through, the screen asks the donor to type in their email address.
  • Step 4 - Check Email: If they enter their email, the system sends them a message asking them to create a donor profile.
  • Step 5 - Finish Setup: The donor fills out their name and address online. Once this is done, the card they used is saved to their profile, so any future donations with that card will automatically be tracked.

Using a Different Card

If a donor uses a different credit or debit card at the kiosk in the future, they will have to enter their email address again to link the new card to their profile.

Common Causes for Missing Tax Receipts

Donors frequently inquire about unissued tax receipts from kiosk transactions. This typically occurs due to the following reasons:

  • Bypassing the Email Prompt: Donors often tap their payment card and leave before entering their email address.
  • Missing Contact Information: Without an email address, the system cannot prompt the donor to provide the mandatory mailing address required for official tax receipts.
  • Incomplete Profile Registration: Entering an email at the kiosk is only the first step. The donor must follow the link sent to their inbox to finish setting up their account. Tax receipts cannot be generated for incomplete profiles.
  • February Issuance Deadline: Consolidated tax receipts are automatically distributed every February. Donor profiles must be completely filled out before this time to ensure they receive their receipt.

Assisting Donors with Missing Receipts

If a donor contacts support regarding an unissued kiosk receipt, guide them through the following recovery steps:

  • Step 1 - Create an Account: Instruct the donor to register for a MAC portal account online. They must use the exact email address they intended to provide at the kiosk.
  • Step 2 - Link the Payment Card: Have the donor securely connect the specific credit or debit card used for the kiosk transaction to their new profile.
  • Step 3 - Escalate to Accounting: If the past donation still does not sync to their dashboard, direct them to use the Report Missing Donation tool within their profile to submit the transaction details to the accounting team.
