Kiosks

Manage self-service donation kiosks and track kiosk transactions.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

QMac Kiosks are large, standalone donation machines found in the head office and MAC mosques. They let people easily make self-service donations by tapping their cards. It is important to understand how they work so you can help donors fix missing tax receipts.

How Donating at a Kiosk Works

When someone uses a kiosk, the process looks like this:

  • Step 1 - Choose a Campaign: The donor picks what they want to support on the screen.
  • Step 2 - Tap to Pay: The donor taps their credit or debit card on the machine to donate
  • Step 3 - Enter Email: After the payment goes through, the screen asks the donor to type in their email address.
  • Step 4 - Check Email: If they enter their email, the system sends them a message asking them to create a donor profile.
  • Step 5 - Finish Setup: The donor fills out their name and address online. Once this is done, the card they used is saved to their profile, so any future donations with that card will automatically be tracked.

The Missing Tax Receipt Problem

Many people ask why they didn't get a tax receipt for their kiosk donation. Here is why it happens:

  • Skipping the Email Screen: About 80% of donors just tap their card and walk away without typing in their email address.
  • No Email Means No Receipt: If the donor does not enter an email, the system cannot ask them for their mailing address. Without an address, a tax receipt cannot be created.
  • February Deadline: Remind donors that all tax receipts are sent out every year in February, so they need to have their profiles completely filled out before then.

How to Help Kiosk Donors

If a donor is missing a tax receipt from a kiosk donation, you can help them fix it:

  • Make an Account: Tell the donor to go online and create a MAC portal account using the exact email address they intended to use at the kiosk.
  • Link the Card: Make sure they connect the credit or debit card they used at the kiosk to their new profile.
  • Report Missing Donation: If the donation still does not show up, tell them to use the Report Missing Donation tool on their profile to submit the details to the accounting team.
