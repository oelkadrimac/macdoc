Clover POS

Point-of-sale system and physical form processing for in-person donations.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

Clover Point of Sale (POS) machines are utilized across various MAC centers to process in-person credit and debit card transactions. It is important to understand the specific manual workflows required to ensure donors who use Clover terminals receive their tax receipts.

System Disconnection

Clover machines are not connected to MACSIS. Unlike the newer Stripe S-700 machines, donations made through Clover terminals will not automatically appear in the system, nor will they automatically show up on a donor's online profile.

Processing Donations for Tax Receipts

Because Clover machines are completely anonymous and only generate paper receipts, center staff must manually collect donor details using an Information Form. (Note: Do not refer to this as a pledge form, as no future pledge is being made).

Follow these steps to ensure the donation is processed correctly by the accounting department:

  • Step 1 - Print Receipt: Process the payment on the Clover machine and print the physical paper receipt.
  • Step 2 - Complete the Information Form: Ask the donor to completely fill out a physical Information Form. Ensure they provide their First Name, Last Name, Address, Phone Number, Email Address, and Company Name (if applicable).
  • Step 3 - Attach the Documents: Staple the printed Clover receipt directly to the donor's completed Information Form.
  • Step 4 - Submit to Accounting: Send the physical package to the accounting department. They will use this collected information to manually add the donation to the donor's profile, allowing a tax receipt to be issued.
