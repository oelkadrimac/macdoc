Managing Credit Card (CC) Donations

Comprehensive guide to managing credit card donations and transactions.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

The Donations module, located under the Finance tab, serves as the central hub for viewing, filtering, and tracking all financial contributions. The CC Donations section specifically displays all online credit card transactions processed through the donation platform.

Accessing the Donations Module

Sign in to the MACSIS Admin Panel using your Microsoft MAC email account at https://sis3admin.macnet.ca/home. Once you have successfully signed in, navigate to the Finance tab in the left-hand navigation menu (identified by the $ icon) and expand it. From there, click Donations to access the available donation modules, including CC Donations and E-Transfers.

Where Do Our Donations Come From?

MACSIS centralizes donations received from all of MAC's supported donation platforms, allowing you to manage and report on contributions from a single location.

Donations can be received through the following channels:

  • Donation Portal –Online donations made through the MAC Donation Portal.
  • Donation Kiosks –Self-service donation kiosks located at participating MAC centres.
  • MAC POS (S700) –In-person donations processed through the MAC S700 point-of-sale terminals.

Regardless of where a donation is made, all transactions are synchronized with MACSIS, providing a centralized location to view, manage, verify, and report on donations across your centre.
