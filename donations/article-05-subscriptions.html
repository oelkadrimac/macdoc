Subscriptions

Manage recurring donations and subscription settings.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

The Subscriptions module allows you to track, monitor, and manage recurring donations set up by donors to provide steady, ongoing support for MAC initiatives over scheduled intervals.

What Is a Subscription?

A subscription represents an automated recurring donation plan created by a donor. Subscription entries feature distinct visual identifiers on the backend dashboard:

  • Wi-Fi Icon: Subscription rows display a small Wi-Fi icon next to their ID to easily differentiate them from one-time contributions.
  • Numerical Multiplier: Subscriptions display a multiplier representing the billing frequency structure (e.g., Donation Amount * 1 indicates a monthly donation).

How Do I View Subscriptions?

To view all active and past recurring plans, navigate to Finance → Subscriptions.

The Subscriptions page displays a centralized table containing:

  • ID (with Wi-Fi icon and status markers)
  • Program Name
  • Description / Campaign
  • Frequency
  • Amount
  • Start Date and End Date
  • Status (Active, Ended, Cancelled)

Managing Subscription Cancellations

When a recurring donor wishes to stop their automated giving plan, the cancellation follows a streamlined procedure:

  • Donor-Initiated Request: Donors can submit a cancellation request directly through their online profile portal.
  • Admin Review & Processing: The Admin Team can view pending cancellation requests and manage the schedule from the donations and finance dashboard.
  • Automated Confirmation: Once the subscription has been successfully canceled by the administrator, the system automatically triggers a confirmation email to the donor.
