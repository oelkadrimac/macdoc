Enrollment

Manage student enrollment and enrollment records.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

The Enrollment tabs within the student management panel allow administrators to transition qualified candidates—transferred from the Applicants section first—into active school rosters, track student placements, and manage active versus completed enrollment statuses.

What is the difference between Enrolled and Registered?

  • Enrolled: Holds students who have been transferred from the Applicants section through the admissions pipeline and processed via the "Enroll" action from the Ready to Enroll tab. Students can be enrolled even if their payment is still pending (triggering a confirmation prompt noting that some students haven't completed payment yet). The table in this tab includes an Enroll column displaying specific class, grade, subject, and section details (e.g., bostonGrade01 bostonsubject01 bostonSection01). From here, administrators can perform further actions like registering students, transferring schools, assigning homerooms, or adding charges.
  • Registered: Represents students who have fully completed all official registration and final onboarding steps in the system.

How do I enroll students from the "Ready to Enroll" tab?

  1. Navigate to the Ready to Enroll tab under the student management panel (where candidates arrive after being transferred from the Applicants section).
  2. Select the checkbox next to the desired student record(s).
  3. Open the Select Action dropdown menu at the top-left of the table and choose Enroll.
  4. Click the Apply button.
  5. Class & Section Assignment: You will be prompted to assign the student to specific class, grade, subject, and section details (e.g., bostonGrade01 bostonsubject01 bostonSection01). You can enroll students in multiple classes using this assignment workflow.

Payment Warning Prompt

If any selected student has not yet completed their payment, a confirmation modal will appear stating: "There are 1 or more students who haven't completed the payment yet. Do you want to perform the 'Enroll' action.?"

Click OK to proceed or Cancel to abort.

What actions are available in the Ready to Enroll tab?

Using the Select Action dropdown menu in the Ready to Enroll tab, administrators can execute operations such as:

  • Enroll: Moves candidates into the enrollment phase and assigns class, grade, subject, and section details.
  • Re-Enroll: Re-enrolls existing or returning student records. When triggered, a notification popup appears stating "Re-Enrolment Initiated. You will get a mail shortly. You may export the log.", which includes an option to download/export the activity log.
  • Withdraw: Closes and archives student applications.
  • Transfer: Transfers student records to another school.
  • Add Charge: Applies financial charges to student accounts.
  • Download Profile: Downloads student profile documentation.
  • Download Documents: Downloads uploaded files and documents associated with the student.
  • Move To Wait List: Reassigns students back to the waiting list.

What actions and features are available in the Enrolled tab?

In the Enrolled tab, administrators can manage active student enrollments and view key details such as student ID, name, class placement details under the Enroll column, gender, email, language, age, applied date, grade applied, and payment status.

The Actions column provides quick access icons for:

  • Opening student communication/internal notes.
  • Viewing and editing the full student profile.
  • Downloading student data and documents.

Additionally, selecting records in the Enrolled tab and opening the Select Action dropdown provides options such as:

  • Enroll
  • Register
  • Withdraw
  • Transfer: Transfers student records to another school.
  • Assign Homeroom
  • Add Charge
  • Export Enrollments
  • Download Profile
  • Download Documents
Note: Ensure class assignments and payment statuses are reviewed regularly during the enrollment and registration workflows.
