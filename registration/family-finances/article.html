Family Finances & Charges

Manage household billing records, family details, and payment workflows.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

The Family Detail page acts as the central financial and household management hub in MACSIS. Accessible via the Finance section within a student profile, this view allows administrators to oversee household billing, sibling discounts, collection methods, and transactional records across all family members enrolled in the school.

How do I access family financial details?

To open the Family Detail financial dashboard:

  • Open an individual student profile from the student application panel.
  • Locate the bottom quick-navigation section, click on the Finance tab, and select the Charges link.

What is displayed in the Household Overview?

The top header section of the Family Detail page summarizes core household information:

  • Parent & Address Information: Displays the names and contact details for the Father and Mother, alongside the shared household Address.
  • Students List: Lists all sibling student records linked to the household within the school.
  • Student Records Link: Administrators can use the Manage Students link to quickly adjust student associations.
  • EFT Details Status: Displays the electronic funds transfer verification status with an edit option.

How do I add and manage charges?

The main Charges tab organizes all billing line items associated with the family:

  • Adding New Charges: Click the plus sign (+) operation button located at the top-right corner of the charges table to open the Add Charge modal. Complete the required fields—including Select Student*, Charges Type*, Amount, and Due Date*—along with any optional Remarks, then click Add.
  • Table Directory: Tracks essential data including Charge Id, Charge Type, Student Name, Amount, Discount, Balance, Due Date, School Year, Status, BC Status, and Invoice For.
  • Individual Actions: Update payment routing methods for individual charges by clicking the action buttons on the right side of the table row to Modify Collection Method (supporting options such as Cash, Cheque, Debit/Credit Card, Education Fund, and Credit Card).
  • Action Menu Options: Using the Select Action dropdown menu above the table, administrators can perform actions on a single item or across multiple selected items in bulk:
    • Cancel: Cancels selected or individual charges.
    • Modify Discount: Applies or adjusts discounts (such as sibling discounts).
    • Remove Discount: Clears previously applied discounts.
    • Send Email: Dispatches billing notices to parents.
Note: For any type of discounts, you MUST contact the Accounting Team.

What other financial tabs are available?

The lower section of the page features specialized tabs for comprehensive auditing and tracking:

  • Payments: Tracks historical payment records, transaction numbers, payment types, amounts, and statuses.
  • Credit Memo: Displays issued credit memos, invoice balances, and memo amounts.
  • Refunds: Details processed refunds, refund dates, and transaction references.
  • Orphan Payments: Houses unallocated payments that require manual matching to specific charges.
  • Orphan Credit Memos: Lists unassigned credit memos awaiting review and allocation.
