School Activities

Oversee and manage school activities and programs.

By MAC Administration Last updated:
Video Walkthrough: School Activities Module & Parent Portal Workflow (Phase 1).

The School Activities module is a comprehensive administrative tool designed to create, track, and manage various educational programs, extracurriculars, or special events that require specific billing or enrollment tracking. To access this area, navigate to the left-hand menu, expand the Administration module, and select School Activities.

Navigating the Activities Dashboard

The main All School Activities dashboard displays a structured table containing all of your existing programs and events.

  • Data Columns: The dashboard provides a clear overview of each activity's unique ID, Name, Description, associated fee Amount, and current Active status.
  • Search and Filter Tools: Use the Search bar in the top right corner to quickly locate specific activities. You can also adjust the items displayed per page using the pagination controls at the bottom right to view more records at once.
  • Quick Actions: Under the Actions column on the far right of the table, you can utilize quick-access icons to manage records:
    • Edit: Click the grey pencil icon to modify an existing activity's details.
    • Delete: Click the orange trash can icon to remove an activity from the system.
Figure 1: All School Activities dashboard displaying the activity table, search bar, and quick-action icons.

Adding a New School Activity

To create a new activity and integrate it into the registration and billing system, follow these steps:

  • From the All School Activities dashboard, click the blue + icon located in the top right corner of the screen.
  • The Add School Activity side panel will open. Fill out the required parameters. Please note that fields marked with an asterisk (*) are mandatory.

Activity Fields and Parameters:

  • Activity Name*: Enter the formal title of the event or program (e.g., test_activity).
  • Description*: Provide necessary details regarding what the activity entails, target grade levels, or prerequisites.
  • Amount*: Input the monetary fee required to participate in this activity.
  • From date: Use the calendar icon to select the official start date of the activity.
  • To date: Use the calendar icon to select the official end date of the activity.
  • Status: Ensure the Active box is checked if you want this activity to be immediately available and visible in the system for registration.

Click the Add button at the bottom right of the panel to save the new activity, or click Cancel to discard the entry.

Accessing Activity Details and Participant Management

Once a school activity has been successfully created, administrators can navigate to a detailed view to manage and track which students are participating in that specific program.

  • Accessing the View: You can open the Student Activity Details page by clicking directly on the activity's ID number from the main activities list.
  • Summary Panel: The top of the details page features a summary panel displaying key metrics, including the activity's Name, Amount, Total Amount Collected, Status, Description, and Start Date.
Figure 2: Student Activity Details page displaying participant tracking tabs and the Add Students selection window.

Viewing and Tracking the Student List

Directly below the summary panel is the Student List section, which organizes participant data and streamlines tracking workflows.

  • Status Tabs: The section includes dedicated tabs labeled Sent, Accepted, and Rejected to help administrators monitor student enrollment statuses at a glance.
  • Comprehensive Data Columns: The participant table displays detailed information for each student, including their Student ID, Last Name, First Name, Gender, Age, Email, and Phone number.

Adding Students to an Activity

To assign and enroll students into an activity manually, follow these steps:

  • Click the blue Add Students button located on the top right side of the Student List area.
  • An Add Students window will pop up, displaying a complete directory of available students in the system.
  • Filtering by Class: To narrow down the list, click the Class Filter dropdown menu at the top left to select specific class groups.
  • Searching for Individuals: Alternatively, you can utilize the Search bar located on the top right to look up specific students by name or ID.
  • Select the desired students by clicking the corresponding checkboxes located on the left side of each row next to their Student ID.
  • Once all required students are selected, click the Add button in the top right corner of the window to confirm your selection and close the panel.

The Parent Experience & Portal Workflow

Once students are invited by administrators, parents complete the process through email notifications and the MAC Portal:

  • Email Notifications: Parents receive an automated email notification stating that a new activity has been created for their child, outlining the event details, fee amount, and a direct link to view details.
  • MAC Portal Access: Clicking the link directs parents to log into the MAC Portal straight to the activity invite details page.
  • Parent Action Options: From the portal, parents can choose from three primary actions:
    • Pay: Parents can complete the secure payment process by entering their card details. Once paid, the activity status updates to accepted.
    • Reject: Declining the activity moves the entry directly to the parent's Rejected tab.
    • Cancel: Returns the parent back to the list of activities without making changes.
