MACSIS Administration Guide: Managing Payment Errors and Resolving Failed Transactions

This comprehensive guide explains how administrators can navigate the Payment Errors dashboard, investigate why transactions fail, resolve billing issues on family accounts, manage Business Central synchronization blocks, and clear items from the administrative workflow queue.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

1. Understanding How Payment Errors Occur

When parents or guardians submit payments online through the MACSIS portal, the system processes transactions securely via Stripe. However, transactions can occasionally fail due to expired credit cards, insufficient funds, or communication timeouts with the payment gateway.

Furthermore, once a payment or invoice is generated in MACSIS, it automatically syncs with Business Central (BC)—the organization's dedicated accounting processor—for official ledger posting and financial approval. If this synchronization fails, a system error is flagged, requiring administrative intervention.

2. Navigating to the Payment Errors Dashboard and Filtering Queues

The Payment Errors dashboard is your primary command center for tracking down unresolved financial issues:

  • Locate and expand the Finance module on the left-hand navigation sidebar.
  • Click on Payment Errors to launch the main error management interface.
  • Look at the top left corner of the dashboard and locate the "Select Type" dropdown menu.
  • Filter your view based on the type of transaction failure: Invoice Payment Error (for tuition, program costs, or ancillary school charges) or Donation Payment Error (for voluntary family contributions or campaign donations).
Figure 1: Payment Errors dashboard showing error queue filters and type selection.

3. Investigating Error Messages and Technical Logs

Before applying a fix to a family's account, you must examine the specific root cause of the error:

  • Ensure you are viewing the Outstanding tab at the top of the dashboard to see active, unhandled errors.
  • Locate the specific error row and click the small blue "i" icon found in the Error Message column.
  • Review the Payment Error Details popup window that appears, which displays critical metadata including the Invoice ID, Payment ID, student name, parent contacts, charge type name, and total amount.
  • Click the "Click here for more technical details" link at the bottom of the popup to expand the Stripe Error Json and Stripe Response Json logs for IT escalation.
  • Click the red OK button to close the details window.

4. Recognizing Business Central (BC) Integration Status Icons

Clicking directly on the blue Invoice ID number within the error queue redirects you instantly to the corresponding Family Detail page. Navigate to the Charges tab to inspect billing history and look for Business Central integration status icons:

  • 🔴 Red BC Icon: Indicates a synchronization failure. The charge exists in MACSIS, but it failed to push through to Business Central, blocking accounting ledger posting.
  • 🟢 Green BC Icon: Indicates a successful integration. The invoice has synchronized cleanly with Business Central.
Figure 2: Payment Error Details window displaying technical JSON logs and metadata.

5. Resolving Failed Charges on the Family Detail Page

Once you have identified a failed charge with a red BC sync error, locate the target charge row on the Family Detail page and click the three-dot icon (...) in the far-right Actions column to open the adjustment menu:

  • Pay: Process an immediate manual payment using alternative funds to clear the outstanding balance.
  • Add Credit Memo: Apply an existing pre-paid account credit to offset and clear the charge.
  • Add Discount: Open a prompt to apply an approved financial reduction to the invoice.
  • Convert to June Invoice: Push the billing cycle forward for that specific invoice.
  • Add Comments: Type an internal note documenting why the transaction failed or steps taken to fix it.
  • Add Collection Method: Assign or modify the payment instrument attached to the invoice.
  • Send Email: Open an automated communication window to message parents directly regarding the billing block.

6. Updating Outdated or Declined Collection Methods

If a payment failed because a parent's credit card expired or their account was declined:

  • Locate the collection method indicator badge (such as Cheque, Cash, or Credit Card) displayed next to the Actions menu on the charge row.
  • Click directly on the badge to open the Update Collection Method popup window.
  • Open the dropdown menu and select the correct payment method (e.g., Cash, Cheque, Debit/Credit Card, Education Fund).
  • Save your selection to update the file and re-attempt processing.

7. Acknowledging and Clearing Resolved Errors from the Queue

After resolving a charge or communicating a fix to parents, you must clear the item from your active queue:

  • Return to the main Payment Errors dashboard and verify you are on the Outstanding tab.
  • Find the error you just fixed and click the green arrow located in the Actions column.
  • An "Add Message" popup box will appear. You must type a brief explanation into the Comment\* field (e.g., "Contacted parent; updated credit card information").
  • Click the blue OK button to archive the item under the Acknowledged tab.
Figure 3: Family Detail page showing charge actions, BC integration icons, and collection method badges.

8. Managing Error Workflows and Reverting Items

As your administrative team processes daily errors, track progress across dashboard tabs:

  • Acknowledged Tab: Houses errors that have been reviewed, investigated, or fixed. If an error was accidentally acknowledged, click the yellow arrow in the Actions column, provide a mandatory comment, and the item will move back to Outstanding.
  • All Tab: Provides a comprehensive historical log of every payment error across all statuses, featuring a yellow "Outstanding" badge for active items.

9. Best Practices for Escalating Issues to the Accounting Team

If a transaction error is tied to a complex system bug, escalate it to your internal finance department with the following information:

  • The Unique Invoice Number: Navigate to the Family Detail page, locate the charge, and if the BC icon is green, click the icon to copy the exact invoice number.
  • Student & Family Name: Ensure the student's full name is clearly stated.
  • Technical JSON Logs: Copy the Stripe error logs from the blue "i" icon on the main Payment Errors dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • What does a Red BC icon mean next to a student's charge?
    A red BC icon indicates an integration error, meaning the charge failed to synchronize properly with Business Central for accounting ledger posting.
  • How do I clear an error from my main Outstanding queue?
    Once you have resolved the charge or contacted the family, click the green arrow in the Actions column on the Payment Errors dashboard, enter a mandatory explanation note, and click OK.
  • Can I revert an error from the Acknowledged list back to Outstanding?
    Yes. Go to the Acknowledged tab, click the yellow arrow in the Actions column, provide a comment in the popup window, and it will return to the outstanding queue.
  • Where can I find technical data to escalate a persistent payment bug?
    Click the blue "i" icon in the Error Message column on the Payment Errors dashboard, then click the link for technical details to view the Stripe Error Json and Stripe Response Json.
