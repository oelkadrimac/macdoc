Managing Charges and Discounts

This guide covers how administrators can configure system charges and discounts, and apply them directly to student records within MACSIS.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

Before assigning any fee or discount to a student, the item must first exist in your system's master list.

Adding a New Charge Type

  • Navigate to the Administration side tab bar and click on Charge Type.
  • Click the + icon to open the "Add Charge Type" window.
  • Fill in the Charge Name and Description.
  • Toggle the selection to mark it as an Ancillary Fee or Program Fee.
  • Input the Amount, select the Collection Method (e.g., Cheque, Cash, EFT), and choose the Project Code.
  • Click Add to save the new charge type.

Adding a New Discount

  • From the same Administration > Charge Type menu, switch to the Discount tab.
  • Click the + icon to open the "Add Discount" window.
  • Select the discount category from the dropdown menu.
  • Fill in the Discount Name and Description.
  • Toggle the switch to select either a flat Amount or a Percentage, and enter the specific value.
  • Choose whether this discount applies on registration and click Add.

Verifying Active Discounts

Before billing, review the master list to ensure specific discounts (e.g., 5%, 10%, 25%, Sibling combinations, MAC Education Funds) are active. If a required percentage is missing, submit a ticket to IT Support to have that discount tier enabled.

Student Profiles – Applying Charges and Discounts

Once the discount or charge is active, assign it directly to the student. Navigate to Students Info > Students, and click the Payment icon on the student's application row.

Managing the Charges Table

When managing a student's finances, the Select Action dropdown menu above the table is your primary tool for making adjustments.

  • Cancel: Cancels the selected charge completely.
  • Modify Collection Method: Changes how the fee will be collected (e.g., switching from EFT to Cheque).
  • Modify Discount: Opens a window to apply or change a discount code.
  • Remove Discount: Strips an existing discount from the selected charge, reverting it to the full amount.
  • Send Email: Triggers an email communication to the parent regarding the specific charge.

To execute updates using the table tools:

  • Adding a Charge: Click the + icon on the far right. Select the student, choose the Charges Type, input the Amount and Due Date, add Remarks, and click Add.
  • Applying a Discount: Check the box next to the charge row. Click the Select Action dropdown and choose Modify Discount. Select the discount and click Apply Discount.
