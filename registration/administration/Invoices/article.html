Invoice Validation and Quality Assurance

Review quality assurance validation tabs, handle pre-submission errors, and manage refunds and NSF protocols.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

Quality Assurance – Invoice Validations

Review the Validations tab before submitting any invoices. The system will automatically block submissions if critical errors are present.

Validation Tab Definition & Required Action
Invalid Salesperson Code CRITICAL ERROR. A class is missing its code. Administrators cannot fix this. Immediately submit an IT Support ticket.
Withdrawn Student Flags withdrawn/waitlisted students. Action: Ignore; no action required.
Unlinked Client / Donors Flags missing constituent (CT) or FT numbers. Action: Ignore; Accounting handles this.
Unverified EFT SHOWSTOPPER. Invoices will fail to submit. Action: Open the attached void check/direct deposit form, confirm details match, check the verification box, and click Verify.
Duplicate EFT Details Flags identical banking info (often siblings). Action: Verify they are siblings. Contact parents if unsure.
Duplicate Constituents/Customers Two parents share a CT number or students share an FT number. Action: Escalate to Accounting before submitting invoices.
Invalid EFT Details Formatting errors (e.g., entering "000"). Action: Change Collection Method to "Cheque" to allow submission, then contact the parent for updated EFT info.

Managing Refunds and Non-Sufficient Funds (NSF)

  • Credit Card (Stripe) Refunds: Administrators can manually process a refund in MACSIS if the transaction occurred within the last 30 days and falls within the current calendar year.
  • EFT Refunds: Administrators cannot process EFT refunds directly. Click the Request Refund button on the student's finance profile, then send a separate email to the Accounting team including the principal's written approval for the refund.
  • NSF (Bounced Checks): Do not manually refund or process a bounced check. Accounting receives an NSF report and updates MACSIS centrally. The invoice will automatically revert to an "Unpaid" status on your end, re-enabling the action buttons to collect a new payment.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • What should I do if I encounter an "Invalid Salesperson Code" error?
    This is a critical system error that administrators cannot fix manually. You must submit an IT support ticket immediately.
  • How do I bypass an "Unverified EFT" block to submit invoices?
    Open the attached void check or direct deposit form, confirm the details match the family's records, check the verification box, and click Verify.
  • Can I process an EFT refund directly in MACSIS?
    No. You must click the Request Refund button on the student's finance profile and email the Accounting team with the principal's written approval.
  • How are bounced checks (NSF) handled in the system?
    Do not process them manually. Accounting handles NSF reports centrally, which automatically reverts the invoice to an "Unpaid" status so you can collect a new payment.
