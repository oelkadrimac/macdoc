Administration

Administrative tools and settings for student registration.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

The Administration section is where you configure and manage the core settings that support your school's registration and payment processes within MACSIS. From this area, administrators can create and maintain charges, configure payment types, manage discounts, organize system codes, and oversee school activities. These settings help ensure registrations, payments, and day-to-day administrative operations are configured accurately and function smoothly throughout the system.
