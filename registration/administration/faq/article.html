FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the administration section.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

General System & Invoice Workflows

Q: Can I change the collection method or apply a discount after an invoice is submitted and posted (green circle)?
A: No, once posted, you cannot modify, discount, or pay it manually. Action buttons only re-enable if the payment returns as NSF.

Q: How can I track which invoices haven't been paid for a specific month?
A: Navigate to the Fee Tracker, select Review Invoices, and check the Unpaid Invoices list.

Q: What should I do if my submitted invoices are stuck in "Awaiting Post" (Gray)?
A: If it is past the 5th of the month, follow up directly with Accounting using the specific Invoice Number (found by clicking the green icon under the BC Status column).

Q: If an unpaid invoice from a previous month is still in the system, will it automatically be collected in the current month's EFT file?
A: No. You must contact IT or Accounting to have them change the date to the 1st of the next month so it can be collected in the upcoming batch.

Q: What date should we set for a new invoice if the 1st of the current month has already passed?
A: You must set it for the 1st of the next month, as the system will not allow past-date submissions.

Q: Should we use the bulk "Send Email" action in the Charges tab to remind parents to pay?
A: No, this feature is not intended for bulk monthly invoice reminders.

Discounts and Education Funds

Q: Who enables the MAC Education Fund (MEF) discount code initially?
A: IT and Accounting must enable the code in the master list before admins can assign it.

Q: When the MEF is approved, do we add it ourselves on MACSIS, or does Accounting do it?
A: School admins must add the discount to the students themselves once approved by the board.

Q: What should we do if we are waiting on MEF approval and the invoice deadline is approaching?
A: Do not base billing on last year's paperwork. Wait for the National School Board to send the official list of approved applicants.

Q: Our school usually implements Education Funds later in the year (e.g., October). Is this allowed?
A: Timing variations must be discussed and verified directly with the National School Board representative.

Q: How do we apply discounts for full-time staff children?
A: Go to Administration > Charge Types > Discounts and assign the "MAC employee discount" to the child. Look for combination codes (e.g., "Sibling 2 with employee 25") if they are also a sibling.

Q: The sibling discount is currently showing as a flat $25, but the board approved a 5% discount. Can we change it?
A: School admins cannot manually change established amounts. Accounting must create the new parameters.

Class Sections

Q: If we have different sections of a grade (like 4A and 4B), do we need to specify that in MACSIS?
A: Separating into sections is strictly for internal organization, not accounting, unless there is a tuition fee difference.

Q: Why does the system require a "Class Limit" for each section?
A: It controls registration numbers. If you group all students into one grade, manually increase the "Class Limit" to accommodate them, or new students will be waitlisted.
