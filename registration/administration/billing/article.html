Billing & Invoice

Manage billing and invoicing for registrations.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

1. Table Action Buttons and Quick Navigation

When navigating the administrative dashboards and data tables, you will primarily use the quick action buttons located on the table rows.

  • The Edit Button: Click this button on a student's row to open their individual profile. You will use this to view uploaded banking documents, modify discounts, or update program information.
  • The Submit Button: This button is used at the end of your workflow to send finalized invoices to Business Central (the accounting processor).

2. Verifying Parent Banking Information & Invoice Numbers

Before any billing can occur, you must ensure the banking data entered into the system matches the physical documents provided by the parents.

  • Verification Steps: Click the Edit button to open a student's file and compare the typed system data to the uploaded void cheque or direct deposit form. You must verify the Transit Number (usually 5 digits), the Institution Number (usually 3 digits), and the Account Number. Replace any old data if new documents have been uploaded.
  • "00" Numbers (Unverified): If a student's invoice number begins with "00", the account is unverified. The system will block you from submitting these invoices.
  • "FT" Numbers (Verified): Once you successfully verify the banking information, the status indicator will turn green. The system will then automatically remove the "00" prefix and generate a permanent "FT" number, signaling the invoice is ready for processing.

3. Submitting Monthly and One-Time Invoices

There are two distinct types of invoices you must submit depending on the fees being collected for that billing cycle.

Path A: Submitting Monthly Invoices (Program Fees)
These invoices are strictly for recurring, monthly tuition fees.

  • Navigate to Finance > Submit Invoices > Monthly Invoices.
  • Select the current billing month (e.g., August) from the dropdown.
  • Click the Submit Monthly Invoices button located at the top right.

Path B: Submitting One-Time Invoices (Ancillary & Resource Fees)
These invoices are for isolated charges like resource fees, beginning-of-the-year supply fees, or extracurricular activities.

  • Navigate to Finance > Submit Invoices > One-Time Invoices.
  • Select the relevant parameters for the one-time fees you are collecting.
  • Click the submit button to push the invoices to accounting.

4. The Post-Submission "Not Sent" Check (CRITICAL)

After submitting either type of invoice, navigate to the Not Sent tab to double-check that it is empty. If Accounting deletes a flawed invoice on their end, it will quietly revert to this tab.

5. Monitoring Invoice Statuses

Track invoice progress by the status colors displayed in the system:

  • Gray (Awaiting Post): Submitted successfully, awaiting Accounting team processing.
  • Green (Posted): Approved and posted by Accounting. You will see a green icon under the BC Status column. Clicking this green icon reveals the specific invoice number used to communicate with the Accounting team. Note: Once posted, you cannot modify the collection method or pay the invoice directly.
  • Red (Error): Failed to process; review the error message.

Managing Program Adjustments

If a student transfers between programs with different tuition rates (e.g., Regular to Al-Furqan):

  • Transferring to a Higher-Fee Program: Do not alter the original base invoice. Add a secondary charge for the monetary difference (e.g., an extra $100) for every remaining month. Include a descriptive remark (e.g., "Extra fee for moving to Al-Furqan").
  • Transferring to a Lower-Fee Program: Create a Credit Memo for the difference in the fee amount for every remaining month, and submit those memos to Accounting.
