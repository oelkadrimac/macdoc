FAQ

Frequently asked questions about student registration.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

1. Student Medical Records & Health Management

Where do I access student medical records and health tags?

Student medical records are located under Students Info > Medical Concern in the sidebar navigation. The interface offers a Normal View for detailed student cards and a printer-friendly Print View optimized for physical distribution or emergency binders.

What filtering options are available on the Medical Concern dashboard?

Administrators can use class filters, status filters, and medical concerns multi-select options to narrow down records by allergies, EpiPen needs, asthma, and immunization statuses.

How do I update a student's medical information?

Open an individual student profile, click on the Health & Medical tab in the top navigation bar, and click the Edit button in the top right corner to modify health details.

2. Waiting Lists & Advance Registrations

How do I access and manage the waiting list?

Navigate to Student Info > Students and select the Waitlist tab. Excess registrations over maximum capacity are automatically queued onto the waiting list.

What happens when an applicant is moved to the waiting list?

Moving a student triggers an automated email with a waitlist-invoice link, setting the application status to Incomplete until paid. Previously paid application fees remain non-refundable.

What is the purpose of the Future Waitlist?

The Future Waitlist module handles requests from families who want to register for an upcoming academic session well in advance. It allows long-term planning by capturing registration requests for future sessions (e.g., submitting a registration request for the 2028-29 session while the current active session is 2026-2027), keeping prospective future enrollments organized separately from current active rosters until the relevant enrollment period opens.

3. Family Finances & Charges

How do I access family financial details and household billing?

Open an individual student profile, locate the bottom quick-navigation section, click on the Finance tab, and select the Charges link.

How do I add new charges to a family household?

Click the plus sign (`+`) button at the top-right corner of the charges table to open the Add Charge modal, complete the required fields, and click Add.

How are discounts and collection methods managed?

Administrators can modify payment collection methods using table row action buttons. For any type of discounts, administrators MUST contact the Accounting Team.

4. Enrollment & Registration Workflows

What is the difference between Enrolled and Registered students?

Enrolled students have cleared required fees and been assigned to specific classes, grades, and sections, whereas Registered students have fully completed all official registration and final onboarding steps.

How do I enroll students from the Ready to Enroll tab?

Navigate to the Ready to Enroll tab, select the student record(s), open the Select Action dropdown, choose "Enroll", and click "Apply" to assign class and section details.

5. Incomplete Registrations & Document Requests

How do I manage incomplete student applications?

The Incomplete section allows administrators to review records and add internal comments using the comment bubble icon under the Actions column. The Requested to Resubmit tab tracks applications flagged for necessary document corrections.

How do I request missing documents from an applicant?

Open the student profile, navigate to the "Documents" tab, and use the "Request Document(s)"" function to select required files, which can be tracked centrally under the Requested Documents tab.
