Enrollment

Manage student enrollment and enrollment records in PowerSchool.

By MAC Administration Last updated:

1. PowerSchool Integration and Enrollment Sync

This module connects your MACSIS registration data directly to PowerSchool, ensuring enrollment statuses and contact details remain strictly aligned.

Phase 1: MACSIS Enrollment Prerequisites

Before syncing, ensure your foundational project settings are configured correctly to capture the right data. Navigate to the Projects Setup module on the left sidebar.

  • Activate the Allow Registration toggle to permit new enrollments to be processed.
  • Configure any necessary enrollment restrictions for specific student groups.
  • Activate the Semesters and Levels toggles to capture mandatory academic structures.

Phase 2: Configuring PowerSchool Settings

Define the baseline rules for how MACSIS statuses translate into official PowerSchool records.

  • Navigate to Integrations > Power School > Settings.
  • Check the Active box to enable the PowerSchool sync functionality.
  • Select the eligible MACSIS statuses by checking Enrolled and/or Registered.
  • Define the default Enrollment Status, Entry date, Exit date, School Number, School Id, and applicable Programmes using the text fields and dropdowns.
  • Click Save Changes.

Phase 3: Processing the Sync

Push MACSIS registrants into PowerSchool via the Sync dashboard located at Integrations > Power School > Sync. Select students by checking the boxes next to their names, then choose an action from the dropdown menu.

  • New Tab: Select freshly registered students and choose Sync to generate their initial PowerSchool profiles.
  • Returning Tab: Select existing students and choose Re-Sync to update their historical profiles with new term data.
  • Synced Tab: Manage officially enrolled students. Choose Sync new contacts to push newly added family information, or choose Re Sync to update a modified core profile.
Figure 1: PowerSchool Sync dashboard displaying the New, Returning, and Synced status tabs.

Managing Individual Contacts

From the Synced tab, you can manually manage specific parent or guardian data transfers.

  • Click the blue View Contacts button next to an enrolled student to open their Contact List panel.
  • Click the blue Sync button next to a specific family member to push their data to PowerSchool.
  • Verify completion by checking the action column; successfully transferred profiles will display an Already Synced status.

2. Managing Individual Student Enrollments

Administrators can manually adjust specific class assignments directly from a student's profile outside of the mass system sync process.

Enrolling a Student

  • Click a student's ID from any directory list to open their Student Profile.
  • Click the Enrollments tab located in the lower-left panel.
  • Click the blue Manage Enrollments link.
  • Open the Class dropdown menu on the Enroll Student page.
  • Check the boxes for the desired classes and click the blue Enroll button.

Unenrolling a Student

  • Navigate to the same Enroll Student page via the student's profile.
  • Locate the active schedule table at the bottom of the page.
  • Click the blue Unenroll button under the Actions column next to the relevant class.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • What if I try to enroll a student in a class that is already full?
    The system will trigger a capacity warning pop-up stating, "Class limit has already reached. Do yo want to enroll the student into this class?". You can override this limit by clicking the red OK button, or click Cancel to abort the enrollment.
  • What if a student's profile is updated in MACSIS after they are already synced?
    The student will automatically be flagged and appear under the Out of Sync tab on the PowerSchool Sync dashboard. You must select their profile and perform a fresh Re-Sync to ensure PowerSchool reflects the newest data.
  • What if a sync fails to transfer properly?
    The affected student will appear in the Sync Errors tab. Review the Sync Errors or Duplicate Errors columns to identify the specific data conflict, correct the issue in MACSIS, and attempt the sync action again.
  • Why aren't any new students appearing in the registration portal?
    Ensure that the "Allow Registration" toggle is active under your primary Projects Setup settings. If this is deactivated, the enrollment form is locked and no new profiles can be generated.
  • How can I tell if a parent's contact info is already in PowerSchool?
    Navigate to the Synced tab on the PowerSchool Sync dashboard and click "View Contacts" next to the student's name. If the parent's data has successfully transferred, the action button will read "Already Synced" instead of offering a Sync option.
